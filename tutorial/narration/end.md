That is Lumen, from an empty ESP32 to a complete, local lighting system.

We flashed a board and secured it, added lights of every type, grouped them into rooms, captured scenes, and drew a live floor plan.

We built automations that run without the internet, explored the hardware tools, connected Home Assistant, linked a second controller over ESP-NOW, and looked at networking, security and safe updates.

Everything runs on the controller. If the internet goes down, or the broker, or even your Wi-Fi, the lights keep working.

The firmware and the web app are open source. You will find the code, the documentation and ready-to-flash releases on GitHub, linked in the description.

If you build your own board profile, or try Lumen on new hardware, I would love to hear how it goes.

Thanks for watching.
